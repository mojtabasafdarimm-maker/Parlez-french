# Curriculum and mnemonic rules

The actual learning target is direct **picture → spoken French recall** for an adult A1/A1.2 beginner. On card front there should be NO words by default. Nouns include un/une/des and gender, actions use correct conjugation, and phrases should be natural in French.

## Lessons discussed
Clothing and what to wear in contexts (interview, sport, winter), adjective order, daily routine/reflexives, weather, time, objects/material/function, and tense contrasts: future proche (`Je vais manger`), present continuous (`Je suis en train de manger`), recent past (`Je viens de manger`). Also connectors: parce que, pour, avec, mais, sans; and beginner aller/venir paradigms. The handoff ZIP includes **one original 55-page A1.2 course PDF**; this is not a complete record of all past lesson material.

## Hints
After an explicit request, present an authentic Persian/English mnemonic, then an optional short scene cue, then partial French start. Specific example: Persian **برس** ↔ `brosse` (brush teeth); **شومیز** ↔ `chemise`; **کوافور** ↔ `coiffeur / se coiffer`. Explain associations as memory aids; do **not** claim etymological origin without evidence. Review all 50 seeded cards in `public/app.js` and hints in `public/hints.js` for forced associations, accurate accents/pronunciation and visual scene semantics.

## Visual correctness
Visual scenarios must distinguish `Je me réveille` vs `Je me lève`, hair combing vs tooth brushing, mittens vs gloves, before/now/after eating and 2:15 vs 2:30 vs 2:45. Make the action inferable in 3 seconds without English text.

## Library edits
Do not overwrite user-added or user-edited cards while correcting seed content. Existing study progress and multiple profiles must remain valid.
